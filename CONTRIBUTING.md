# Contributing to OverCut

Stub: a fuller guide arrives with the first external-contribution milestone. Read `CLAUDE.md` (workflow), `docs/PRODUCT.md` and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` first.

## Licence and sign-off (DCO, no CLA)
Your contribution is licensed under the licence of the part you change (see `LICENSE`): GPL-3.0-or-later for `apps/*`, MIT OR Apache-2.0 for `crates/*` and `packages/*`. You keep your copyright. There is no CLA.

Every commit needs a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off (https://developercertificate.org), added with `git commit -s`:

```
Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
```

By signing off you certify that you wrote the change or have the right to submit it under the licence above.

## Process
One change per pull request, branch from `main`, CI (`verify`) green before merge. Features follow the milestone plan in `docs/ROADMAP.md`; anything else starts as an issue.
