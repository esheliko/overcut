# Dragging clips on the timeline (M2)

**Behaviour.** Everything is a pure function in `apps/web/src/timeline/arrange.ts`, called by the gesture machine (`interact.ts`); the canvas only draws.
- **Move.** Mouse: press a clip and drag (the clip is selected on press). Touch: a tap selects, a drag on a *selected* clip moves it, a drag on an unselected clip pans; **press and hold ~0.4 s** on an unselected clip picks it up (short buzz where the device has one), and the next drag moves it. Keyboard: the existing nudge keys (`,` `.`, Shift = 10 frames).
- **Snap.** The selection's first start and last end are pulled onto the nearest clip edge on any track, the playhead and 0:00 within 8 px; a dashed accent line shows the snap point. `snapMove` is the single place to swap in the engine's snapping later.
- **Reorder.** Past the middle of a neighbouring clip the selection *jumps over it* (one neighbour per middle passed, either direction): the jumped clips pack against the side the selection left. A picture and its linked sound hop together while their neighbours are equally long; otherwise the move just stops against the neighbour as before. A guide line shows where the selection will land. Release applies park/shift/settle moves as ONE undo step.
- **From the media bin.** Drag a card onto the timeline canvas: a guide line follows the pointer (snapped like a move); dropping places the clip there, or after the last clip when that spot is taken (`placeAt`). Dropping elsewhere on the timeline panel still uses the playhead.

**Edge cases.** Locked tracks never move (a selection that is all locked says so). A hop is refused (plain move) when an unselected clip lies between selected ones on a track. Positions stay whole frames except where a snap target is not on a frame (a clip edge from another source).

**Tests.** `tests/unit/arrange.test.ts` (snap, hop planning, op replay, long press), `tests/smoke/drag-move.spec.ts` (mouse reorder + undo, short drag does not reorder).

## Edit tools: ripple, roll, slip, slide (TL-04, M2-045)
Planning in `timeline/ripple.ts` (pure, `tests/unit/timeline-ripple.test.ts`), gestures in `interact.ts` (`k: "tool"`), ops from the engine (`timeline-ops.md`). Every drag ends in ONE op list = one undo step; a linked partner on an unlocked track gets the same edit in the same list, and the allowed range is what ALL of them allow.
- **Without a mode switch (desktop).** Hold **Alt** and drag: an edge shared by two touching clips **rolls** the cut; any other clip edge **ripple-trims** (the rest of its track follows); a clip body **slips** (drag right = earlier source, like dragging the picture); **Alt+Shift** on a body **slides** it between its neighbours.
- **Tool picker (touch and mouse).** ONE toolbar row: undo, redo | split, delete, ripple delete | a **tool picker** button (shows the current tool, tinted while it is not Select; opens Select / Ripple / Roll / Slip / Slide with their keys V B N Y U) | **More** | zoom. Everything else (duplicate, copy, cut, paste, unlink, close gap, clip timing, history, add track, track options, zoom) is a labelled row of the More menu (a popover on wide screens, a bottom sheet on phones); a row that cannot act says why ("Select a clip first"). On phones the bar keeps only Fit for zoom (pinch + More cover the rest). **Esc** on the timeline returns to Select; the cursor is a crosshair while another tool is active. With a tool picked, a drag of a clip edge (ripple, roll) or body (slip, slide) does that edit; a tap still selects.
- **Buttons and keys.** Ripple delete (`Shift+Delete`) deletes the selection and pulls the track in; Close gap (`Ctrl+Delete`) closes the empty stretch before the selection (the first selected clip's group). Keyboard by frames: slip `Ctrl+,` `Ctrl+.`, slide `Ctrl+Shift+,` `.`, roll the end cut `Ctrl+Alt+,` `.`. All are in the registry, so they appear in the shortcuts dialog (group "Edit tools") and can be remapped.
- **Limits.** Drags snap to whole frames inside the range the engine would accept (a clip keeps one frame, stays inside its media, a slide cannot overrun a non-touching neighbour); the preview shows the new edge (ripple), the new cut (roll) or the moved ghost with its amount (slip, slide). No timeline snapping to other edges yet.
- **Not here.** Numeric entry for these edits (the timing dialog covers start/length), sync-lock across tracks (`all_tracks` is never sent; links are mirrored instead).
